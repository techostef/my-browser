Privacy Policy for OBrowser

App package: com.techoky.obrowser
Last updated: June 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the OBrowser mobile application (the "App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. OBrowser is a web browser that lets you browse websites and download media (videos, images, and manga) to your device. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

In short: OBrowser does not require an account and does not have its own servers. Your browsing activity, search history, tabs, settings, and downloaded files are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. The App does use certain third-party services (such as Google AdMob for advertising) that may collect data, as described below.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data stored only on your device

The following information is created and stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or any server we control:

  • Browsing data — web pages you visit, browser tabs, and website cookies/site data held by the in-app web view.
  • Search history — search terms you enter in the address bar.
  • Downloaded files — videos, images, audio, and manga you choose to download, saved to your device storage and media library.
  • App settings and preferences — including language and feature settings.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing it within the App or by uninstalling the App.

1.2 Data collected by third-party services

The App integrates the following third-party services, which may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

ServicePurposeData it may collect
Google AdMob Displaying advertisements within the App. Advertising identifier (Ad ID), device information, IP address, and ad-interaction data, used to serve and measure ads.
Google Play Services / In-App Updates Checking for and delivering App updates. Device and app version information needed to check for updates.
OpenAI (optional) AI-powered subtitle translation, only if you enable it and provide your own OpenAI API key. The subtitle/text content you choose to translate is sent to OpenAI for processing. This feature is off by default.
AI features: Subtitle transcription (speech-to-text) runs entirely on your device; audio is not uploaded. Only the optional translation step sends text to OpenAI, and only when you have entered your own API key and started a translation. If you never set up this feature, no data is sent to OpenAI.

2. Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • Internet / Network access — to load web pages and download files.
  • Media / Storage access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — to save downloaded media to your device and to display and play the videos and images the App has downloaded. The App accesses media files only to provide its download, viewing, and playback features; it does not upload your media anywhere.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — for in-app overlays such as the video player and detection banner.
  • Vibrate — for in-app feedback and notifications.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to:

  • Provide browsing, media detection, download, and playback features.
  • Remember your tabs, history, and preferences on your device.
  • Display advertisements (via Google AdMob).
  • Deliver app updates.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not collect data on our own servers.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not share your information with third parties except through the third-party services listed in Section 1.2, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google (AdMob & Play Services): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenAI (optional feature): https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because data is stored locally on your device, it remains until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. To request deletion of data held by third-party services, please refer to those services' privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Since OBrowser stores data locally and does not maintain user accounts, you can exercise most of these rights directly by clearing data within the App or uninstalling it. For data held by third-party services, contact those providers directly.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
